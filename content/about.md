Hi, I'm Miguel! I'm a Senior Software Engineer at [Lyft](https://www.lyft.com/) in New York,
and I've spent the last nine years or so working in Infrastructure, building the systems that other
engineers rely on to ship code and keep it running.

From 2018 to 2024 I was on the Deploys team, where I helped design
[Lyft's Continuous Deployment](https://eng.lyft.com/continuous-deployment-at-lyft-9b457314771a) system.
Today more than 95% of Lyft's services use it to ship to production automatically.
I helped build Facets and FacetController, an abstraction and controller that let us roll out
infrastructure changes across the whole fleet safely.
In 2024 I moved to the Compute team, where I work on our Kubernetes platform.
I lead our automated resource rightsizing work so that services get the CPU and memory they actually need.
I was also one of the shared execution leads across the Infrastructure teams for Lyft's launch in Europe.
